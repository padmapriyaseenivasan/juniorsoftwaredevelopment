The Laundry Management System is a web-based application designed
to modernize and automate the daily operations of a laundry business. In traditional
laundry services, most activities such as order booking, tracking garments, managing customer details, and handling payments are performed manually using paper-based records or basic spreadsheets. These outdated methods are often inefficient, time-consuming, and highly prone to human errors such as data misplacement, incorrect billing, and difficulty in tracking order status
. As businesses grow, managing large volumes of customer and order data manually becomes increasingly complex and unreliable.
